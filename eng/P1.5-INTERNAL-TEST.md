### Prize cannot be topped up

There is no top-up function on `NovaDemoPool` or `NovaSignal`. A direct mint of `5000000` micro-USDC onto the pool (a stray token transfer) left the prize ledger at `900000`. `conserved()` stayed `true`. Pool token balance rose to `5980000`; that extra balance is not prize.

```text
PRIZE_BEFORE_STRAY=900000
PRIZE_AFTER_STRAY=900000
CONSERVED true
```

`releaseNovaBudget` from the owner (not the signal contract) reverted with `NotSignal()` (`0xc91046ec`). A further `signal(1)` after the budget was spent reverted `BudgetExceeded()`. Prize stayed `900000`. Temp wallet balance stayed `20000`.

`signal` cannot name the prize pool as recipient. Spending the operating budget does not change `prizePool`.
